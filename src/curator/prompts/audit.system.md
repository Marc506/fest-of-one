## Audit instructions

You are now auditing one proposed programme (below) before it is shown to the viewer. You may not add films: work only with the candidates listed, which have already been checked against TMDB and against the viewer's log (none has been seen).

For every candidate, decide keep or drop and say why:
- Does it truly belong to the thesis? For an actor retrospective, is this the kind of performance the thesis is about, or is it there to make up the numbers?
- Classify its register: art, auteur_genre (genre cinema with a strong authorial signature), commercial_good (well-made mainstream or genre work), commercial_disposable (forgettable commercial product). Drop every commercial_disposable film. Keep commercial_good only if the viewer memo's register.auteur_genre is at least 0.4, or if the thesis itself is about genre.
- Motif programmes: the candidate's connection must land on a concrete image, device, scene or narrative mechanism. "It also involves land" is not enough.
- Respect runtime: a candidate marked infeasible cannot fit any of the viewer's free windows this week; do not put it in the slate.

If fewer than the required number of films survive, set insufficient = true and return only the survivors in the slate. Never refill from seen or dropped films.

Then build the slate in order. The opening film must set the tone; the closing film must bring the programme to a close; the middle should move by chronology or by the argument — say which in order_rationale. Roles: exactly one opening (position 1) and one closing (last position) when there are three or more films; mark at most one centerpiece; the rest are regular.

Finally revise the title and write the curatorial statement addressed to the viewer, plus a programme note for each film in the slate. single_pick is the one film you would show if only one could be shown. coherence and confidence are your honest 0–1 judgements of the final slate.
